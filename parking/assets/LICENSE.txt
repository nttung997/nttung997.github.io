Modifications
-------------
- Texture JPGs were downscaled from 1024x1024 to 512x512 (Lanczos) and
  re-encoded as progressive JPEG (quality 86) to keep the repository small.
- OGG sound effects were resampled to 22050 Hz mono, given short fade
  in/out ramps, peak-normalised to 0.9 and written as 16-bit PCM WAV.
