Two parent lines converge and a life begins. For a long while those lines walk beside it, almost as one, then they drift away. Another line draws near and stays beside this one until both end. Along the way a new line branches off, at first the colour of its parents, then its own, and it continues past the edge of the frame.

Function of Life Of two lines, a life; of a life, a line that goes on.
